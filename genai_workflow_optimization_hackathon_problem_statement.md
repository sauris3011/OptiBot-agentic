# GenAI Workflow Optimization Hackathon — Problem Statement

## Description

Organizations across industries are rapidly adopting Generative AI to transform business processes, enhance employee productivity, improve customer experience, and accelerate innovation. However, as GenAI solutions move from pilots to enterprise-scale adoption, many organizations struggle to consistently realize sustainable value, measurable ROI, and trustworthy outcomes.

This hackathon use case challenges participants to select a meaningful business scenario from any industry, domain, or enterprise function and demonstrate how the effectiveness of a GenAI-enabled workflow can be improved through optimization, governance, efficiency, and responsible adoption. The solution should make AI interactions more cost-efficient, reliable, secure, measurable, and business-aligned.

## Problem Statement

Many GenAI implementations are deployed without adequate focus on prompt efficiency, retrieval quality, response accuracy, guardrails, security controls, operational governance, and cost optimization. As adoption scales, these gaps can result in unnecessary token and compute spend, inconsistent or low-quality responses, hallucinations, compliance risks, poor user trust, and limited business impact.

Participants must design and demonstrate an optimized GenAI workflow that improves the business effectiveness of an existing or proposed AI-driven process. The solution should show how GenAI can be made more efficient, governed, measurable, and user-centric while maintaining alignment to business objectives and responsible AI expectations.

## Data Requirements

Participants may use anonymized, public, sample, or synthetically generated data (similar to real data) as relevant to the selected business scenario. The dataset should be sufficient to demonstrate before-and-after improvement in AI effectiveness and should support evaluation across performance, trust, cost, governance, and user experience dimensions.

| Data Category | Examples | Purpose in Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Business process data | Tickets, cases, documents, policy FAQs, SOPs, customer queries, knowledge articles, service requests, transaction logs | To define the target workflow and validate business relevance. |
| Knowledge base / retrieval data | Curated documents, indexed repositories, domain knowledge, document chunks, metadata, search logs, citations | To improve retrieval accuracy, contextual grounding, and answer traceability. |
| Prompt and interaction data | Baseline prompts, optimized prompts, conversation samples, prompt templates, user personas, task instructions | To compare prompt effectiveness, token usage, repeatability, and user experience. |
| Performance and cost metrics | Token consumption, latency, compute utilization, cost per interaction, response time, throughput | To quantify efficiency gains and cost optimization. |
| Quality and governance metrics | Accuracy scores, hallucination checks, toxicity/safety results, compliance flags, access controls, audit logs | To demonstrate trustworthy, secure, and governed adoption. |

**Data quality expectations:** Data should be relevant to the problem context, appropriately anonymized, free from sensitive personal information unless properly protected, and adequate to support measurable comparison between baseline and optimized AI behavior.

## Expected Outcome

The final submission should clearly demonstrate measurable improvement in GenAI / AI ML effectiveness. Participants should present a before-and-after view showing how optimization and governance interventions improved the selected workflow.

- Improved prompt effectiveness, reduced rework, and lower token consumption without compromising response quality.
- Improved knowledge retrieval quality through better chunking, metadata, ranking, grounding, or retrieval-augmented generation techniques.
- Higher response reliability, fewer hallucinations, clearer citations or evidence, and improved user trust.
- Reduced cloud, compute, or model usage cost through prompt optimization, caching, routing, batching, model selection, or usage controls.
- A measurable business impact view covering productivity, cycle time, quality, adoption, risk reduction, or ROI.
- A responsible AI and governance view showing security, compliance, monitoring, auditability, and human oversight.
- Improved user experience—enhancing how humans interact with the AI to ensure high adoption rates and workflow integration.

## Expected Solution

Participants are expected to build a working prototype or demonstrable solution that showcases an optimized GenAI workflow for the selected business scenario. The solution should not only generate AI responses, but also prove how the workflow has been made more efficient, reliable, governed, and value-driven.

| Solution Component | Minimum Expectation |
|---|---|
| Working prototype | A functioning demo of the optimized GenAI workflow, chatbot, assistant, knowledge solution, automation flow, or decision-support tool. |
| Before/after analysis | Clear baseline versus optimized comparison using metrics such as token cost, latency, accuracy, hallucination rate, retrieval precision, user effort, or cycle time. |
| Architecture overview | A high-level flow showing user interaction, data sources, retrieval layer, model layer, guardrails, monitoring, and governance controls. |
| Governance and security guardrails | Controls for access, data privacy, responsible AI, prompt safety, response validation, audit logging, human-in-the-loop review, and compliance monitoring. |
| Business value case | A concise explanation of productivity gain, cost saving, quality improvement, risk reduction, adoption benefit, or ROI potential. |
| User experience | A user-friendly interface or workflow demonstrating ease of use, clear feedback, error handling, escalation or fallback options, and fit into day-to-day work. |

## Suggested Evaluation Lens

- **Performance & Efficiency:** Prompt quality, token reduction, latency improvement, retrieval effectiveness, and resource utilization.
- **Trust & Governance:** Accuracy, hallucination reduction, data protection, access controls, compliance, auditability, and oversight.
- **Value & Operations:** Cost optimization, productivity improvement, business ROI, scalability, and operational monitoring.
- **User Experience:** Adoption potential, ease of use, workflow integration, fallback design, and transparency for the business user.

Participants are encouraged to explore creative, practical, and measurable solutions that make GenAI adoption more trusted, efficient, scalable, and business-outcome led.
